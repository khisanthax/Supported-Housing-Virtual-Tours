# Owner approval for the first agency

Keep your copy of this list in a private place. Send the agency the [short setup packet](AGENCY-PILOT.md) and [test checklist](PILOT.md) after approval.

## Before you invite the agency

- [ ] Check the license. Nonprofit housing or social service groups, public housing authorities, and public housing programs may be eligible. For-profit groups need a written paid license before use. [Approved license](https://github.com/khisanthax/leveltwo360-cloudflare-template/blob/774273166e49846c6a2e06dd1b00a35a824a93a4/LICENSE)
- [ ] Name one pilot contact at the agency and one person they can ask for help. Add the work contact details to the private invite.
- [ ] Check that the agency controls its Cloudflare account and private GitHub project. Name the people who can manage both. Make sure another approved staff member can take over.
- [ ] Name the first site Administrator and the person who will back up and restore the site. Make sure that person can use the [corrected backup tool](PILOT-MAINTENANCE.md).
- [ ] Check that the agency has a safe place to store backups. Keep the recovery keys in a separate safe place. Give access only to the people who need it.
- [ ] Approve the agency, contact, and start of the test. Limit it to Workers Free, made-up names, and sample photos. Ask the team to stop if any step fails.

## Before you allow real apartment content

- [ ] Read the agency’s test results. Check that setup, staff limits, guest access and removal, backup, restore, update, and rollback all passed.
- [ ] Confirm the agency can recover its site with its own people, files, and keys.
- [ ] Confirm the agency has agreed to its rules for photos, consent, and who can view a tour.
- [ ] Record separate approval from the agency and the pilot owner before adding real content. Until then, keep the site for tests only.

**Current status** The packet is ready for review. The agency, work contacts, and start date still need the owner’s approval.
