Your agency. Your apartments. Your website.

Give staff and invited guests a guided, private 360° apartment tour. Your agency owns its Cloudflare account, website, images and staff accounts.

  1. Connect to Cloudflare

1. Connect to Cloudflare

Cloudflare handles account creation and sign-in. LevelTwo360 never asks for your Cloudflare password or an API key. Choose Create account on Cloudflare’s sign-in page if needed.

For this pilot, use Workers Free. Free hosting has usage limits and is not unlimited. Setup never adds a payment method or upgrades your plan.

Connect to Cloudflare

Authorize access only to the agency account you intend to use. This service uses a temporary deployment authorization and requests no continuing refresh access.

2. Choose your installation

Keep this name for retries. Each name creates an independent installation. Your final website address includes a unique suffix.

The service also verifies the plan before creating resources. A Free website plan alone is insufficient. No paid services are activated.

Free allowances include 100,000 Worker requests/day, D1 5 million rows read and 100,000 written/day, and KV 1,000 writes/day. These are shared with other apps on your account. Quota exhaustion can stop service.

3. Create your Administrator

Your resources are prepared. Continue on your agency website to choose a username and password. The deployment service never receives that password.

Continue agency setup

Setup authorization lasts ten minutes. If it expires or you refresh the Administrator form before finishing, reconnect here using the same account and installation name.

Need help? Keep the account and installation name. Never send passwords, authorization links, API keys or backup passwords to support.
