Help & Getting Started

Your agency operates this installation. There is no built-in telephone/live-chat help desk or guaranteed response time. Ask your designated Administrator first.

Sign in and get access

Use your individual username and password. An Administrator creates accounts and resets passwords; there is no public registration. Never send passwords, guest phrases or backup passwords in a support ticket. If a session expires, sign in again. A disabled account needs Administrator assistance.

Create an apartment and upload stitched photos

In Apartment Gallery choose NEW APARTMENT and name its private draft. On desktop, Step 1 accepts completed stitched 2:1 JPEGs and ZIPs. Preview and name viewpoints before saving. JPEGs must be at most 4 MiB and 8192 × 4096; apartments support 12 viewpoints. Capture frames must be stitched externally first.

On phones/tablets, use Uploads Gallery. An Administrator or individually designated Staff Supervisor uses Manage Uploads to select which authorized apartments accept photos. Removing a card never deletes its photos. Uploads add Unplaced viewpoints and never publish.

Connect rooms, preview and publish

Desktop Step 2 shows the panorama. Drag a destination handle onto the actual doorway. Place the return separately; do not guess its location. Click an arrow to inspect, drag to move, or double-click to delete the exact pair after confirmation. Undo restores recent changes. Step 3 reviews the relationship diagram; new panorama tours do not infer doors from adjacency. Step 4 previews visitor navigation and lists required fixes. Publish explicitly only after review and agency approval.

Guest Links

Choose published apartments in Guest Links or Group / Mass Share. New links default to three calendar months. Copy Invitation includes the required phrase; Copy Link alone requires sending the phrase separately. Email opens an external mail application. The site sends no email directly. Revocation and permission changes end guest access; disconnected offline copies have separate limits.

Staff permissions

Administrators use User Access Control for role defaults, individual Allow/Deny overrides and apartment scope. An individual Allow never bypasses scope. Supervisor is a Staff account with the individual Manage Uploads Gallery permission. Only Administrators manage users, permissions and diagnostics. Reload after a policy change; the server applies it on the next protected request.

Encrypted backups and restoration

Export Backup asks for a separate password and confirmation. Choose a filename label that is safe to expose: names outside encryption are visible. Keep the password separately; it cannot be recovered. The .shtour.zip archive restores through Virtual Tours, not a normal ZIP password prompt. Older plaintext .shtour files remain readable and require protected storage.

Backups preserve JPEG bytes, rooms, doors, layout, start viewpoint and draft metadata. They exclude staff accounts, roles, guest invitations, audit history, server secrets and full publication history. Rehearse restoration into a new private apartment before replacing or removing work. Full disaster recovery also needs the operator's private D1/KV/configuration backups.

Migration Center

Select up to five portable files totaling 96 MiB. Unlock encrypted files locally, review preview tiles, check only the apartments you intend to import, then choose Migrate Selected and confirm the destination. Every selection becomes a NEW PRIVATE draft; duplicate titles do not replace work. Source files are unchanged. Successful rows stay complete while failed rows can retry with their existing receipt. If the browser closes during an uncertain save, check the Gallery before reselecting the file. This is import, not automatic synchronization.

Offline viewing

Use the existing Available Offline page to download selected apartments and verify readiness. Protect the device and vault password. Test installation, restart without network, all room transitions, reconnection and updates on the intended device. Browser storage can be evicted. A disconnected device cannot receive immediate revocation; local expiry and agency handling rules still apply. Offline authoring is not included.

Common errors and recovery
  • 401: sign in again; disabled/expired sessions cannot continue.
  • 403: ask an Administrator to check permissions, apartment scope and Uploads membership.
  • 409: another draft or selection revision changed. Reload before retrying.
  • 429: wait for the indicated limit reset. Repeated retries do not bypass it.
  • 503: the cause may be unavailable; new KV photos can take time to propagate. Retry later and retain the operation ID.
  • 507: storage capacity reached. Retain backups before safe cleanup; never delete original images through account tools casually.
  • Wrong backup password or damage: authentication cannot always distinguish these. Retry the known password or restore another dated backup.
Report a problem safely

Copy the VT- operation reference shown with the error. Note what you intended, approximate time, app version, browser family and whether a reload shows the save. An Administrator can search Diagnostics, distinguish server observations from browser-reported events, and Export Diagnostic Report as a portable ZIP/JSON attachment.

Reports exclude photographs and secrets by construction, but contain staff usernames and apartment identifiers. Review the report and share only through an agency-approved support channel. No ticket is sent automatically. Do not attach raw photos, backups or credentials without separate authorization. Logs may be sampled, expired, capped, offline or unavailable; absence of a log does not prove success or failure.
